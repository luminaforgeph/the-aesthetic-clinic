Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

Begin with a consultation. A qualified practitioner can assess your goals, skin and medical history, discuss options and explain suitability before any treatment is recommended.

Can I book before choosing a treatment?

Absolutely. The consultation is designed to help you explore options and understand what may or may not be appropriate.

What should I expect from a consultation?

Expect a conversation about your goals, relevant history, questions, possible options, risks, aftercare and realistic outcomes.

Are results guaranteed?

No treatment can guarantee an outcome. Results, risks and recovery vary by person and treatment. Your practitioner should explain these before proceeding.
