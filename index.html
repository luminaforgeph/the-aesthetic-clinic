06 / Questions

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

Start with a consultation. We'll discuss your goals, relevant history, options and suitability before recommending a treatment.

Do I need an appointment?

Consultations and treatments are planned by appointment. Use the enquiry form to request a conversation.

Will my results look natural?

Our design intent is subtle, individualised care. Outcomes vary and are discussed in detail with the clinician before treatment.

Can I ask questions before booking?

Absolutely. Your consultation is an opportunity to understand the options, risks, aftercare and expectations without pressure.
