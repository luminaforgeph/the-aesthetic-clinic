A few things to know

Before you begin.

How do I know what is right for me?

A consultation is the best place to begin. A qualified practitioner can discuss your goals, relevant medical history, skin, possible options and whether a treatment is appropriate.

Do I need to choose a treatment first?

No. You can request a consultation without knowing which treatment you want. The conversation is intended to help you explore and understand options.

What happens during a consultation?

Expect a conversation about your goals, relevant history, options, benefits, risks, aftercare and realistic expectations. You should have time to ask questions and decide without pressure.

Are results or outcomes guaranteed?

No aesthetic treatment can guarantee results. Outcomes, risks and recovery vary. Appropriate practitioners should explain the relevant details before you choose to proceed.
